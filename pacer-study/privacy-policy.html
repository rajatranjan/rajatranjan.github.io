Privacy Policy

Pacer Study · Effective date: October 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Pacer Study Android app (the "App") collects, uses, and protects information. Pacer Study provides educational content for NEET and Intermediate students. By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Information Collection and Use

We designed Pacer Study to collect as little information as possible.

  • No account or login: You can use the App without creating an account or signing in. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or any other personal details.
  • No ads: The App does not display advertisements, and we do not use your information for advertising.
  • Analytics data: To understand how the App is used and to make it better, we collect non-personal data through a third-party analytics service (see "Third-Party Service Providers" below). This may include:
    • Crash logs and error reports
    • Device identifiers and basic device information (such as device model, operating system version, and app version)
    • App interaction data (such as screens visited, features used, and time spent in the App)

We use this information only to analyze App usage, fix bugs, and improve the App's performance and content. We do not sell or rent this information to anyone.

Log Data

When an error or crash happens in the App, we may collect data and information on your device called Log Data. This Log Data may include your device's Internet Protocol ("IP") address, device name, operating system version, the configuration of the App when using it, the time and date of your use, and other statistics. We use Log Data only to find and fix problems in the App.

Third-Party Service Providers

We use Amplitude, a third-party analytics service, to collect the non-personal data described above. Amplitude processes this data on our behalf to help us understand App usage and performance. Amplitude has its own privacy policy, which explains how it handles data:

  • Amplitude Privacy Policy

Amplitude only has access to this data to perform tasks for us and is not allowed to use it for any other purpose.

Security

We value your trust and use commercially reasonable measures to protect the information collected. However, no method of transmitting data over the internet or storing it electronically is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children's Privacy

Pacer Study is intended for students aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.
