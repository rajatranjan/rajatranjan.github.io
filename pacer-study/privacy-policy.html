Privacy Policy

Pacer Study · Effective date: October 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Pacer Study Android app (the "App") handles your information. Pacer Study provides educational content for NEET and Intermediate students. By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Information Collection and Use

We designed Pacer Study to collect as little information as possible.

  • No account or login: You can use the App without creating an account or signing in. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or any other personal details.
  • No ads: The App does not display advertisements, and we do not use your information for advertising.

The App does not collect, store, or share any personal information. We do not sell or rent any information to anyone.

Log Data

The App does not collect log data itself. If the App crashes and you have chosen to share usage and diagnostics data with Google, Google Play may provide us with anonymous crash reports (such as device model, Android version, and app version). We use these reports only to find and fix problems in the App.

Third-Party Service Providers

The App does not include any third-party analytics, tracking, or advertising services. The App is distributed through Google Play, which may collect information as described in Google's own privacy policy:

  • Google Privacy Policy

If we add any third-party service in the future, we will update this Privacy Policy before that service is used.

Security

We value your trust and use commercially reasonable measures to protect any information we receive. However, no method of transmitting data over the internet or storing it electronically is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children's Privacy

Pacer Study is intended for students aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. We encourage you to review this page periodically. Changes take effect as soon as they are posted.
